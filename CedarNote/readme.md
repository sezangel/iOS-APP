## CedarNote

Your thoughts deserve a quiet place—not an account, a feed, or another subscription.

CedarNote is a calm, privacy-focused notepad for capturing ideas, checklists, journals, and everyday details. Start writing instantly, stay organized, and keep control of your data.

WRITE WITHOUT DISTRACTIONS

• No account, ads, tracking, or audio interruptions
• A clean editor with checklists, photos, and file attachments
• Automatic local saving that keeps your work close at hand

ORGANIZE AND FIND ANYTHING

• Create nested folders and add tags
• Pin important notes and archive finished work
• Recover accidentally deleted notes
• Search titles, content, tags, and folders quickly

PRIVATE AND RECOVERABLE

• Lock CedarNote with device authentication
• Keep locked notes out of unauthenticated search
• Return to earlier edits with built-in version history
• Create complete ZIP backups and restore them safely
• Export your notes anytime as TXT or Markdown

CEDARNOTE PLUS — ONE PURCHASE, NO SUBSCRIPTION

Unlock an optional set of advanced tools:

• Private iCloud sync for your notes and attachments
• Conflict-safe preservation through version history
• On-device OCR for selected images and PDFs
• Search text recognized from documents
• Export polished PDFs with three styled templates

The free CedarNote experience always stays ad-free. Your local notes, version history, TXT and Markdown exports, and complete ZIP backups remain available without CedarNote Plus.

CedarNote is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, and Spanish.

Write your first note in seconds—and keep your ideas yours.
